Privacy Policy for YearDots

Effective date: October 1, 2026

YearDots is a calendar wallpaper application that helps users visualize the year, month, goals, milestones, and important dates on their device wallpaper. We respect your privacy and aim to keep the app simple and privacy-friendly.

1. Information We Collect

YearDots does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, messages, or precise location.

The app may process information locally on your device in order to provide its features, including:

  • Current date, month, and year
  • Wallpaper style and appearance settings
  • Selected colors, backgrounds, and textures
  • Custom element positions and layout preferences
  • Goals, milestones, and important dates entered by you
  • Static or live wallpaper preferences
  • Live wallpaper animation preferences

This information is used to generate and display your wallpaper and is intended to remain stored locally on your device unless a future feature clearly states otherwise.

2. Wallpaper Access

YearDots uses Android wallpaper-related system features to create, preview, apply, and update wallpapers on your device.

If you use Live Wallpaper mode, Android's Live Wallpaper system may run the wallpaper service when needed to display or animate your selected wallpaper.

3. Automatic Wallpaper Updates

YearDots may use Android background scheduling features to update your wallpaper when the date changes. This allows the app to move the current-day indicator to the correct day automatically.

These updates are performed locally on your device and do not require an external calendar API.

4. Internet Connection

Core YearDots calendar and wallpaper features are designed to work without an internet connection.

5. Data Sharing

YearDots does not sell your personal information. The app does not intentionally share the goals, milestones, important dates, wallpaper layouts, or other customization information you enter with third parties.

6. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on standard Android and Google Play platform services for operating-system functionality, app distribution, security, and wallpaper features. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Storage and Deletion

YearDots settings and user-created customization data are primarily stored locally on your device.

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing YearDots app data from Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

8. Children's Privacy

YearDots is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not require an account or intentionally collect personal information, it does not request age information from users.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the app's locally stored information protected. However, no software or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when YearDots features or data practices change. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
