Privacy Policy

App: AI Year Progress Wallpaper

Developer: Veritas Studios

Package: com.veritasstudios.aiyearprogress

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

AI Year Progress Wallpaper is an Android application that helps users track year progress and create customizable calendar and progress wallpapers.

2. Information We Collect

The app does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

The app may use basic device information needed for normal functionality, such as:

  • Android version
  • Screen size and display information
  • App version
  • Device language
  • System date and time

3. Date and Year Progress

The app uses your device's date and time to calculate the current day, year progress, percentage completed, and days remaining.

4. Wallpaper Settings

Wallpaper preferences such as colors, themes, layouts, positions, dot size, spacing, and other visual settings may be stored locally on your device.

5. Wallpaper Access

The app uses Android wallpaper features to let you apply wallpapers to your home screen, lock screen, or both.

The app does not use wallpaper access to read messages, contacts, passwords, or other unrelated personal information.

6. AI Features

The app may include AI-assisted themes, visual styles, or design features.

If a future AI feature requires information to be sent to an external service, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Purchases

Some features may require payment. Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Veritas Studios does not directly receive or store your full credit card or debit card information.

8. Advertising

The app does not display third-party advertising if the published version does not contain an advertising SDK.

9. User-Selected Images

If you choose your own image for a wallpaper or background, the app may access the specific image you select.

Unless otherwise stated in the app, selected images are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to Veritas Studios.

10. Notifications

If the app provides reminders or wallpaper update notifications, you may be asked for notification permission. You can disable notifications at any time in Android settings.

11. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Google may process limited information when providing services such as Google Play and Google Play Billing according to Google's own privacy policies.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Most app settings are stored locally on your device.

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the application.

13. Children's Privacy

AI Year Progress Wallpaper is a general-purpose application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

14. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, services, or privacy practices change.
